# Phase 10 - Integration Testing

## Objective

Validate the complete system under stress, large datasets, and realistic operating conditions.

## Scope

- End-to-end integration testing
- Stress testing
- Large dataset validation
- Performance checks

## Exclusions

- New end-user features
- Major architectural rewrites
- Business intelligence expansion

## Deliverables

- Integration test suite
- Stress test coverage
- Performance validation reports

## Success Criteria

- The system remains stable across long-running scenarios.
- Performance regressions are detected early.
- Completed phases remain backward compatible.
