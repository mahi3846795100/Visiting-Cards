# Phase 4 - Business Information Extraction

## Objective

Convert raw OCR text into structured business contact information.

## Scope

- Company name extraction
- Person name extraction
- Designation extraction
- Phone extraction
- Email extraction
- Website extraction
- Address extraction
- LinkedIn extraction

## Exclusions

- OCR engine changes
- Duplicate detection
- Storage organization
- GUI work
- Capture workflow automation

## Deliverables

- Structured contact model
- Extraction rules and validation logic
- Tests for parsing and normalization

## Success Criteria

- Contact fields are extracted without guessing.
- Invalid or ambiguous values are rejected.
- The extraction layer remains independent from OCR internals.
