# Phase 12 - Business Contact Intelligence System

## Objective

Evolve the scanner into a broader business contact intelligence system with search, CRM, analytics, AI, and relationship management features.

## Scope

- Search
- CRM workflows
- Analytics
- AI-assisted insights
- Relationship management

## Exclusions

- Rebuilding completed scanning phases without need
- Breaking backward compatibility with earlier phases
- Introducing cloud dependency by default

## Deliverables

- Intelligence-oriented application layer
- Search and analytics foundations
- User-facing business insight features

## Success Criteria

- The system supports contact intelligence beyond simple capture.
- Earlier scanning capabilities remain intact.
- New intelligence features are introduced without compromising reliability.
